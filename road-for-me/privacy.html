Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

Road for Me is a rally timing application that runs on the event organizer’s laptop. This policy covers the laptop application and its public website.

Event data and purpose

The application stores driver information entered by the organizer and the times recorded for those drivers. The organizer uses these records to administer rally timing and results.

Storage and sharing

The application keeps event records in its local storage on the laptop and encrypts them at rest. The application does not upload, publish, or send these records to the developer or to third parties. The organizer controls access to the laptop and any separate copies or disclosures the organizer makes.

Retention and requests

The event organizer is responsible for the records it enters and uses, including deciding the legal basis for processing and how long to keep the records. Contact your event organizer for its privacy notice or to request access, correction, or deletion of your records, where applicable. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Website visits

This information website has no forms or site-added analytics. GitHub Pages hosts it and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact

For questions about the software or this policy, contact the developer, Adrian Bosze, at rendszerterv@gmail.com.
